# First hour: the task sheet

For a System 1 design partner. Everything you need is in the README:
<https://github.com/garygentry/system1#readme>. This sheet only says what to try. It doesn't say
how, on purpose: we want to see where the docs fall short.

## How this works

- Use your own repo and the agent you normally use, the way you normally use it.
- **Think out loud.** When you'd ask us something, say the question out loud instead, then carry
  on however you can. Those questions are the most useful thing you'll give us.
- We watch and don't help. If a task stops you for about 10 minutes, say so and move on to the
  next one.
- Stop whenever you like. Nothing here needs to be finished.

## The tasks

Do them in order. Each says what counts as **useful**: a live decision, on your own code, that
you'd act on.

### 1. Get it working

Get System 1 working with your agent, in your repo, starting from the README.

**Done when** your agent can make a live decision in this repo. You decide what that means for
your API key and for what may leave your machine; the README explains both.

### 2. A batch you'd rather not read

Pick something in your repo you'd otherwise check by reading many things one at a time: 20 or
more files, a test log, a stretch of a log file, commit messages, an export of issues. Ask your
agent, in your own words, to go through them and flag the ones that match something you care
about.

**Useful when** you get back a short list you'd act on. Look at three it flagged and three it
didn't, and say whether you agree.

### 3. One out of many

Ask your agent to choose the best fit from a long list, where you know what a good answer looks
like: the file to change for a bug report, the dependency that does a job, the test that covers a
function, the right label for an issue.

**Useful when** its pick is one you'd have made, or a better one, and you didn't have to read
the list yourself.

### 4. Is it done? (Claude Code and Codex)

Write down two or three criteria for a small change you want in this repo. Have System 1 check
your agent's work against them when it stops, then ask the agent for the change.

**Useful when** the check let finished work through, or held unfinished work back for a reason
you agree with.

### 4. Where would it pay off? (Pi)

Pi has no hooks, so the stop check above isn't available. Instead, ask System 1 where in this
repo a decision model would pay off.

**Useful when** at least one suggestion is something you'd consider changing. Claude Code and
Codex users: try this too if there's time.
